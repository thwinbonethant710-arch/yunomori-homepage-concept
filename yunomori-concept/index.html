01 The Experience

An escape from the pace of everyday life.

Born in Bangkok, Yunomori is Southeast Asia’s first authentic onsen spa, blending time-honoured Japanese bathing traditions with Thai healing culture.

Soak first. Let the warmth settle the body and quiet the mind. Then let the hands of traditional massage do the rest.

Yunomori — “hot water in the forest”

02 The Onsen

Mineral-rich
waters.

Yunomori’s onsens use real mineral spring water sourced from Wat Wangkanai in Kanchanaburi.

Warmed muscles and a quiet mind leave the body more receptive to the benefits of traditional massage.

03 Spa & Wellness

After the water, the stillness.

Pair the onsen with expertly delivered Thai and aromatherapy massages to release deep tension and reawaken the senses.

  • Traditional Thai massage
  • Herbal compress
  • Head, shoulder & foot massage
  • Deep tissue massage
  • Aromatherapy massage

Services vary by location.
